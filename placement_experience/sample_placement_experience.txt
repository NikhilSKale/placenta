Selection Process:
Round 1: Online Assessment (60 mins)
- 20 Aptitude & Logical Reasoning MCQs
- 10 Core CS Fundamentals (DBMS, OS, OOPs)
- 2 Coding Questions:
  1. Reverse Words in a String
  2. Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters

Round 2: Technical Interview (45 mins)
- Explain Final Year Project architecture.
- Difference between B-Tree and B+ Tree indexing in PostgreSQL.
- SQL query to find 2nd highest salary using window functions (`DENSE_RANK()`).
- Live coding: Implement LRU Cache or explain HashMap collision resolution.

Round 3: HR & Management Round (20 mins)
- Behavioral questions (STAR format): "Tell me about a challenging conflict in a team project."
- Willingness to relocate.
- Discussion on compensation breakdown and joining date.

Tips for Juniors:
- Focus heavily on Data Structures, OOP principles, and clean SQL queries.
- Be thorough with everything mentioned on your resume.
